**Short answer:** The entry edition of professional BIM software costs **$1,530 to $3,005 a seat per year** on annual billing: Vectorworks Architect $1,530, Archicad Studio $2,414, Allplan Concept $2,892 and Autodesk Revit $3,005. AutoCAD, which includes the Architecture toolset, costs $2,095 a year. The cheapest routes are **FreeCAD** (free and open source), **SketchUp** (free for non-commercial use, Pro $399 a year) and **Rhino**, a one-time $995 licence. Prices were checked on each vendor's US store on November 15, 2026.

## Which tools can you still buy outright?

Only **Rhino** (a $995 permanent licence with no maintenance fee) and **BricsCAD** (a lifetime licence plus optional yearly maintenance) still sell one-time licences among the tools we track. Allplan stopped selling new perpetual licences on September 30, 2026. Revit, AutoCAD, Archicad, Vectorworks and Chief Architect are subscription only for new customers.

## Which is the best value?

- **No budget:** FreeCAD, which includes a BIM workbench and reads and writes IFC.
- **Concept design and client models:** SketchUp Pro at $399 a year.
- **Lowest-cost full BIM on Mac or Windows:** Vectorworks Architect at $1,530 a year.
- **BIM on a Mac with a large user base:** Archicad Studio at $2,414 a year.
- **Industry-standard BIM for teams that share Revit models:** Revit at $3,005 a year.
- **You want to own the software:** Rhino ($995) for freeform design, or BricsCAD BIM for DWG-based BIM.
