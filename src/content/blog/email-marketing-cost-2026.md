**Short answer:** For a list of **1,000 contacts**, the entry paid plan of the 10 email marketing tools we priced costs **$16 to $39 a month** on monthly billing (median $19.50). At **10,000 contacts** the same plans cost **$46 to $189 a month** (median $115). The cheapest at 1,000 contacts is [Moosend](/tools/moosend/pricing/) at $16; at 10,000 contacts it is [Brevo](/tools/brevo/pricing/), at $46 for 40,000 emails a month, followed by [GetResponse](/tools/getresponse/pricing/) at $79. The steepest climb is [ActiveCampaign](/tools/activecampaign/pricing/): $19 at 1,000 contacts, $189 at 10,000. Prices are regular USD list prices from each vendor's own pricing data, checked November 15–18, 2026.

## How is email marketing software priced?

Nearly every tool charges by **list size**: the number of contacts (or subscribers, or "active profiles") you store. Each plan has tiers, such as 500, 1,000, 2,500, 5,000 and 10,000 contacts, and you pay for the tier your list falls into. The monthly email allowance usually scales with it, often at 10 emails per contact.

**Brevo** is the main exception. It prices by **emails sent per month**, not contacts. But on its Starter and Standard plans the email tier also caps how many contacts you can store: the 5,000-email entry tier holds only 500 contacts, and the 10,000-email tier holds 1,500. A 1,000-contact list therefore starts on the 10,000-email tier.

## What hidden costs should you check?

- **What counts as a contact.** MailerLite bills for every address active at any point in the billing cycle. GetResponse's free plan counts unconfirmed and removed contacts. Kit counts only confirmed, active subscribers. Klaviyo bills by "active profiles".
- **Overages.** Constant Contact charges $0.002 per email over your allowance, and Mailchimp bills overages automatically on paid plans when you exceed contact or send limits.
- **Add-ons.** SMS is extra almost everywhere. Brevo Starter shows the Brevo logo on emails unless you pay for an add-on. Transactional email (receipts, password resets) is a paid add-on at Mailchimp (Standard and Premium only) and ActiveCampaign, and a separate MailerSend account at MailerLite; Brevo includes it on every plan.
- **Annual-billing discounts vary.** Paying yearly saves 10% at MailerLite and Brevo, 15% at Constant Contact, about 17% at Kit, 18% at GetResponse, 20% at Moosend and up to 21% at ActiveCampaign. Mailchimp, Klaviyo and Omnisend sell self-serve plans monthly only. Our guide to [annual vs monthly billing](/blog/annual-vs-monthly-billing-savings/) explains when the commitment pays off.

## Which email marketing tool is the best value?

- **Cheapest at 1,000 contacts:** Moosend Pro ($16 a month, $13 billed annually), with no monthly send limit.
- **Cheapest at 10,000 contacts:** Brevo Starter ($46 a month for 40,000 emails), then GetResponse Starter ($79, unlimited sends).
- **Best free plan for a newsletter:** Kit, free up to 10,000 subscribers.
- **Smallest price jump as you grow:** Brevo (2.7×) and Kit (3.6×).
- **Largest price jump:** ActiveCampaign Starter, from $19 to $189 a month.
