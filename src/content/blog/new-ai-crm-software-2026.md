**Short answer:** Five AI-first CRMs launched between November 2025 and November 2026: [SparrowCRM](/tools/sparrowcrm/) (September 2026), [Twenty](/tools/twenty/) 2.0 (April 2026), [Day AI](/tools/day-ai/) (generally available February 2026), [Lightfield](/tools/lightfield/) (November 2025) and [Reevo](/tools/reevo/) (November 2025). **SparrowCRM ranks highest, at #4 of 20** in our [CRM ranking](/best/crm-software/), with a free plan for 3 seats and paid plans from $19 per seat a month. **Twenty is the cheapest**: free to self-host, or $9 per user a month in the cloud. For a 5-person sales team, the new CRMs cost from **$45 a month (Twenty) to $999 a month (Lightfield)**, and Reevo publishes no prices. Prices were read on each vendor's pricing page on November 22, 2026.

## What makes these CRMs different?

Older CRMs expect salespeople to type in contacts, notes and deal stages. All five new CRMs fill in records themselves: they read email, calendar and meeting recordings, then create contacts, log activity and suggest or make deal updates. Each has an AI assistant you can ask about your pipeline. The differences are in price model, calling and who the tool is for.

SparrowCRM's free plan covers 3 seats and 50,000 records, so a team of 3 pays nothing. Lightfield's Starter is $89 per user but only goes to 3 seats; the next plan is $999 a month for the workspace, with 5 capture seats included.

## What does SparrowCRM offer?

SparrowCRM is the third product from SurveySparrow. It covers contacts, companies and deals in Kanban views, with email sync, sequences with per-step open and reply rates, workflows, and AI agents that update records and summarize calls and emails. Growth ($19 per seat annually) adds unlimited seats and meeting transcription. A public API, custom objects and lead routing need Pro ($49). Mobile app access is listed on Enterprise only. See [SparrowCRM pricing](/tools/sparrowcrm/pricing/) or [HubSpot CRM vs SparrowCRM](/compare/hubspot-crm-vs-sparrowcrm/).

## What changed in Twenty 2.0?

Twenty is an open-source CRM (AGPL v3) from Paris. Version 2.0, released April 21, 2026, added MCP and OAuth access for AI clients and a redesigned interface. Every plan has unlimited custom objects, workflows, AI agents, APIs and an MCP server. SSO, custom roles and audit logs need Organization ($39 per user annually). It lists no dialer, email open tracking or mobile apps. See [Twenty pricing](/tools/twenty/pricing/) or [Twenty vs Attio](/compare/twenty-vs-attio/).

## How does Day AI pricing work?

Day AI charges per "agent", one per person. Teammates who only need their meetings and emails captured are free, but every workspace needs at least one Professional ($60 a month annually) or Executive ($200) agent. Pipeline management starts at Professional. It can run as your CRM or on top of Salesforce or HubSpot, drafting updates for review. Day AI announced a $20M Series A led by Sequoia when it went generally available. See [Day AI pricing](/tools/day-ai/pricing/).

## Who are Lightfield and Reevo for?

- **Lightfield**, from the founders of Tome, updates accounts and deals from email and meeting content and records meetings from Starter up. It lists SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA and GDPR. It suits small teams on Starter or funded teams ready for a $999-a-month plan. See [Lightfield pricing](/tools/lightfield/pricing/).
- **Reevo** launched with $80M in funding and combines CRM, prospecting data, sequences, a built-in dialer and meeting recording. It's the only new CRM here with built-in calling and an iOS app for calls, but every plan is quote-only. See [Reevo pricing](/tools/reevo/pricing/).

## Which new CRM should you pick?

- **A free CRM for up to 3 people:** SparrowCRM.
- **The lowest cost, or self-hosting:** Twenty.
- **Your team hates data entry and has many meeting-heavy users:** Day AI, with free capture seats.
- **You need a dialer in the CRM:** Reevo, or see [CRMs with built-in calling](/best/crm-software-with-built-in-calling/).
- **You need HIPAA:** Lightfield lists it on its pricing page.
