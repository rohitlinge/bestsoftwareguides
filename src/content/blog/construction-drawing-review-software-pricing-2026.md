**Short answer:** Entry plans for drawing markup and review tools run from **$21.67 to $67 a user per month** on annual billing, starting with Bluebeam Basics (billed yearly at $260). Dedicated takeoff tools cost much more: **$166 to $349** a user per month for PlanSwift, Togal.AI and STACK Takeoff. Three tools have a permanent free plan: **Planspector, Fieldwire and STACK**. Procore is quote-only. Prices were checked on the vendors' pricing pages on November 15, 2026.

*Disclosure: Planspector is a [featured partner](/disclosure/) of BestSoftwareGuides. Its prices below come from its public pricing page, checked the same way as every other tool.*

## What does a 5-editor team pay per year?

Annual billing, entry plan, five people who mark up or measure.

| Tool | How it adds up | Per year |
|---|---|---|
| Bluebeam Basics | 5 × $260 | **$1,300** |
| Drawboard Projects Team | 5 × $270; guest access included | **$1,350** |
| Bluebeam Core (adds overlay and compare) | 5 × $330 | **$1,650** |
| Planspector Team | 5 × $39 × 12; viewers free | **$2,340** |
| Fieldwire Pro | 5 × $39 × 12 | **$2,340** |
| STACK Build & Operate Essential | 5 × $49 × 12 | **$2,940** |
| Autodesk Forma Build Essentials | 5 × $67 × 12 (reported) | **$4,020** |

The cheapest plan is not always comparable. Bluebeam Basics has no overlay or compare and cannot host review sessions. Fieldwire's free and Pro plans have no RFIs; those need Business Plus at $89. Check the feature you need against the plan, not the brand.

## Who has to pay: editors or everyone?

This matters most when owners, consultants or clients only need to look at drawings.

- **Free viewers or guests:** Planspector (unlimited free viewers on paid plans), Drawboard Projects (guest access included), STACK Takeoff (unlimited viewer seats) and Procore (unlimited users on its volume-based contract).
- **Per user:** Bluebeam, Fieldwire and Autodesk price per user, so check how they treat people who only view.

## Which is the best value?

- **Cheapest per-seat markup and measurement on Windows:** Bluebeam Basics at $260 a year, or Core at $330 if you need overlay and compare.
- **Cheapest browser-based team plan:** Drawboard Projects Team at $270 a year per user.
- **Free plan for a small team:** Planspector (3 editors and 10 viewers) or Fieldwire (5 users, 100 sheets).
- **Field tasks and punch lists:** Fieldwire.
- **Full takeoff and estimating:** PlanSwift on Windows or STACK in the browser; Togal.AI for automated AI takeoff.
