**Short answer:** Per-seat pricing is cheaper for small teams and flat pricing wins as the team grows. At **5 users**, Trello Standard costs $25 a month and ClickUp Unlimited $35, both less than any flat plan. At **25 users**, Basecamp Pro Unlimited's flat **$199 a month** (annual billing, unlimited users) undercuts Asana Starter ($274.75) and Notion Plus ($250), but not ClickUp ($175) or Trello ($125). Basecamp prices were checked on its pricing page on November 15, 2026; the per-seat prices come from our rankings, checked November 14–15, 2026.

## What is the difference between per-seat and flat pricing?

- **Per-seat (per-user) pricing:** you pay a set price for every person on the account. The bill grows in a straight line with the team. Most project management, CRM and note-taker tools work this way.
- **Flat pricing:** one price for the whole account, whatever the head count, sometimes up to a user cap. Basecamp is the best-known example: its Pro Unlimited plan has "no per-user fees".
- **Seat bundles:** a mix of the two. monday.com sells seats in groups and starts at 3 seats; Copy.ai's Chat plan is a flat $24 a month that includes 5 seats.

The break-even team size is simple: **flat price ÷ per-seat price**. Above that number of users, the flat plan is cheaper.

## When does a flat plan become cheaper?

Break-even against Basecamp Pro Unlimited ($199 a month, annual billing):

| Per-seat plan | Price per user | Flat plan is cheaper from |
|---|---|---|
| Asana Starter | $10.99 | **19 users** |
| Notion Plus | $10 | **20 users** |
| monday.com Basic | $9 | **23 users** |
| ClickUp Unlimited | $7 | **29 users** |
| Trello Standard | $5 | **40 users** |

The cheaper per-seat price is, the larger the team has to be before a flat plan pays off. Against Trello, Basecamp only wins at 40 or more users.

## What should you check besides the per-user price?

- **Seat minimums and bundles.** monday.com starts at 3 seats, so one person still pays for three. Fathom's Team plan has a 2-seat minimum.
- **Who counts as a user.** Guests, viewers and free collaborators are often not billed. Trello's free plan allows up to 10 collaborators per workspace; ClickUp's free plan has unlimited members.
- **Caps on flat plans.** A flat price with a user or project cap is a seat bundle in disguise. Check what happens when you reach the cap.
- **Annual vs monthly.** The gap can be large: Basecamp Pro Unlimited costs $329 billed monthly against $199 billed annually. See [annual vs monthly billing](/blog/annual-vs-monthly-billing-savings/).

## Which pricing model should you choose?

| Team | Usually cheapest |
|---|---|
| 1–3 people | A free tier (ClickUp, Trello, Zoho CRM, Freshsales) |
| 5–15 people | A low per-seat plan (Trello, ClickUp) |
| 20–40 people | Compare against a flat plan: Basecamp Pro Unlimited beats Asana and Notion from 19–20 users |
| 40+ people, simple needs | Flat pricing usually wins on price alone |
