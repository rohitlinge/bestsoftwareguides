**Short answer:** Entry paid plans for the AI visibility trackers we priced start at **€29 to $599 a month** on monthly billing. The cheapest are [Otterly.AI](/tools/otterly-ai/pricing/) Lite (€29, 16 prompts), [Promptmonitor](/tools/promptmonitor/pricing/) Starter ($39, 25 prompts) and [Knowatoa](/tools/knowatoa/pricing/) Starter ($59). Only two tools have a standing free option: [Semrush](/tools/semrush-ai-visibility-toolkit/pricing/) (a free account with a demo project) and [AI Search Inspector](/tools/ai-search-inspector/pricing/), which is free open-source software where you pay the AI providers per answer instead. [Profound](/tools/profound/pricing/) now sells only custom Enterprise contracts. Prices are USD list prices (Otterly in EUR) from each vendor's pricing page, checked November 23, 2026.

Disclosure: AI Search Inspector is built by the team behind this site. Its numbers below come from its own pricing page, like every other tool's.

## How are AI visibility tools priced?

Almost every tracker charges by **prompts**: the questions it asks the AI engines for you, usually once a day. Some sell **credits** or **AI answers** instead, where one prompt run on one engine uses one credit, so tracking 50 prompts on 4 engines every day uses about 6,000 answers a month. Three things move the price more than anything else:

1. **How many prompts** the plan includes (16 to 600 on entry plans).
2. **Which engines** are included. Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews are often on higher tiers only.
3. **How often** prompts run: daily, twice a week or weekly.

## Which plans hide the biggest extra costs?

- **Ahrefs Brand Radar** needs an Ahrefs subscription (Lite from $129 a month) and Lite includes only 5 custom prompts. Extra prompt packs cost $50, $100 or $250 a month on top.
- **Semrush** sells the toolkit per domain. Each extra Brand Performance domain is another $99 a month, and 50 extra prompts are $60.
- **Peec AI** includes 3 models; each extra model costs $30, $70 or $140 a month depending on the plan.
- **Rankscale** charges $9 a month per brand dashboard beyond 10 on Pro.
- **Otterly.AI** Lite tracks 4 of 7 engines and gives 3 recommendations a week; the API starts on Standard (€189).

## Which tool should you pick?

- **Lowest price for one brand:** Otterly.AI Lite (€29) or Promptmonitor Starter ($39), if 16 to 25 prompts are enough.
- **Already pay for Semrush or Ahrefs:** start with their add-on so AI data sits next to your SEO data; Semrush is the highest-scoring tool in our ranking.
- **Most engines for the money:** Trakkr Growth covers all 8 models it supports, Claude included, at $100 a month.
- **No subscription:** AI Search Inspector, if you are comfortable creating API keys and paying the providers directly.
- **Enterprise:** Profound, Scrunch Enterprise and Goodie Enterprise sell custom contracts with SSO and dedicated support.
