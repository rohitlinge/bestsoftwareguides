**Short answer:** Entry paid plans for AI writing tools range from **$4.89 a month** (Wordtune Advanced, billed annually) to **$79 a month** (Writesonic Starter), and team or workflow tiers run much higher: Copy.ai's start at $1,000 a month, while Jasper Business and Hypotenuse AI are quote-only. Four of the 10 tools we track have a permanent free plan: **Grammarly, QuillBot, Wordtune and Rytr**. Prices were checked on the vendors' pricing pages on November 15, 2026.

The headline price is only half the story. These tools bill in three different ways, and the billing unit decides what a team really pays.

## How do AI writing tools charge?

| Billing model | Tools | What you pay for |
|---|---|---|
| **Per user (seat)** | Grammarly, QuillBot, Wordtune, Jasper | Each person who uses it |
| **Per account, seats included** | Copy.ai, Anyword, Writesonic | A flat monthly price that covers a set number of users |
| **Per account, credit-based** | Sudowrite | A monthly allowance of AI credits for one account |
| **Quote only** | Hypotenuse AI | A custom contract after a sales call |

Rytr charges per account and publishes monthly prices only; it says yearly billing saves 20% but does not show the yearly figure.

## What does a 5-person team pay per year?

Annual billing, cheapest plan that covers five people, before tax.

| Tool | How it adds up | Per year |
|---|---|---|
| Copy.ai Chat | One $24/month plan includes 5 seats | **$288** |
| Wordtune Advanced | 5 × $4.89/month | **$293** |
| QuillBot Premium | 5 × $99.95/year | **$500** |
| Grammarly Pro | 5 × $144/year | **$720** |
| Anyword Data-Driven | $79/month for 3 seats + 2 extra seats at $49/month | **$2,124** |
| Jasper Pro | Pro includes 1 seat; Jasper asks teams to contact sales for more | Quote |

These plans are not interchangeable. Copy.ai Chat is a multi-model chat workspace, Wordtune and QuillBot rewrite and paraphrase, and Grammarly checks and drafts inside other apps. Price the plan for the job you need done.

## What limits come with the cheaper plans?

The lowest price usually comes with a usage cap.

- **Grammarly:** 100 AI prompts a month on Free, 2,000 per member a month on Pro.
- **Wordtune:** 10 rewrites a day on Basic (free), 30 a day on Advanced, no cap on Unlimited.
- **QuillBot:** 125 words per paraphrase and 2 modes on Free; Premium removes the word limit and adds a plagiarism checker with a monthly cap.
- **Sudowrite:** 225,000 credits a month on Hobby & Student, 1,000,000 on Professional and 2,000,000 on Max.
- **Writesonic:** 15 AI articles a month on Starter, 25 on Basic and 50 on Growth.
- **Rytr:** 10,000 characters a month on Free; paid plans are unlimited.

## Which plan is the best value?

- **Cheapest way to remove free-plan limits:** Wordtune Advanced at $4.89 a month on annual billing.
- **Best all-round value for one person:** Grammarly Pro at $12 a month on annual billing, which adds plagiarism and AI-text detection.
- **Cheapest for a small team that wants a chat workspace:** Copy.ai Chat, a flat $24 a month for 5 seats.
- **Fiction writers:** Sudowrite from $10 a month on annual billing.
