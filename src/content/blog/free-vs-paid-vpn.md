**Short answer:** Of the 10 VPNs we rank, only **Proton VPN** and **Windscribe** have a permanent free plan. Proton's free plan has **no data cap** but allows **1 device**, servers in **10 countries** chosen for you, medium speed, and no streaming or P2P. Windscribe's free plan allows **unlimited devices** but caps data at **10GB a month** (with a confirmed email). Upgrading costs $2.99 a month for Proton VPN Plus's first year (renewing at $83.88 a year) or $5.75 a month for Windscribe Pro on yearly billing. Checked on both vendors' pricing pages on November 15, 2026.

## Which VPNs have a free plan?

| VPN | Free plan | Data | Devices | Locations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Proton VPN](/tools/proton-vpn/) | Yes, no card needed | No limit | 1 | 10 countries, chosen at random |
| [Windscribe](/tools/windscribe/) | Yes | 10GB a month (2GB without a confirmed email) | Unlimited | 10 countries |
| NordVPN, ExpressVPN, Surfshark, CyberGhost, PIA, IPVanish, PureVPN, Mullvad | No | — | — | — |

The other eight sell only paid plans. Most give a 30-day money-back guarantee instead; CyberGhost gives 45 days and Mullvad 14.

## What do you give up on Proton VPN's free plan?

| | Proton Free | Proton VPN Plus |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $0 | $9.99 a month, or $35.88 for the first year (then $83.88 a year) |
| Devices at once | 1 | 10 |
| Servers | 10 countries, chosen for you | Every country, your choice |
| Speed | Medium | Full |
| Streaming and P2P | No | Yes |
| Ad and malware blocker | No | Yes |
| Data limit | None | None |

Proton's free plan suits occasional use on one laptop or phone, such as public Wi-Fi. You can't choose the country, so it is no good for reaching content in one particular country.

## What do you give up on Windscribe's free plan?

| | Windscribe Free | Windscribe Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $0 | $9 a month, or $69 a year ($5.75 a month) |
| Data | 10GB a month with a confirmed email | Unlimited |
| Devices at once | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Locations | 10 countries: US, Canada, UK, Hong Kong, France, Germany, Netherlands, Switzerland, Norway, Romania | All locations |

Windscribe also sells a **Build A Plan** option: $1 per location per month, with a $3 minimum. Each location adds 10GB to your monthly allowance. You can earn extra free data by posting about Windscribe on X (5GB a month) or by referring friends (1GB each).

10GB a month covers browsing and email on public Wi-Fi. It does not cover streaming video: Windscribe's free plan is better for occasional use on many devices, Proton's for steady use on one.

## Is a free VPN safe?

A VPN can see your traffic, so the question is who runs it and whether its no-logs promise has been checked.

- **Proton VPN** has had its no-logs policy audited by Securitum four years running, most recently in August 2025.
- **Windscribe** has had its servers (Packetlabs, 2024) and apps (Leviathan Security Group, 2021 and 2022) audited, but not its no-logs policy as such.

Both free plans come from companies that sell paid plans, which is how the free plan is funded. Before using any free VPN, check whether its company publishes a named audit; the auditor and year for every VPN we rank are on its page.

## When is it worth paying?

| You need | Free is enough? |
|---|---|
| Safer browsing on public Wi-Fi, one device | Yes: Proton Free |
| Light use on several devices | Yes, within 10GB: Windscribe Free |
| Streaming or a server in one particular country | No |
| Every device in a household | No: see [VPNs with unlimited devices](/best/vpns-with-unlimited-devices/) |
| Torrenting (P2P) | No |
