**Short answer:** Paid CRM plans start between **$9 and $25 per user/month** on annual billing. The cheapest is Freshsales Growth at $9, and the most expensive entry tier is Salesforce Starter Suite at $25. The gap opens at mid tiers: Zoho CRM Professional is **$23**, while HubSpot Sales Hub Professional and Salesforce Pro Suite are **$100**.

Prices: USD per user per month, billed annually, last checked November 14, 2026. Pipedrive's tier names and prices have changed recently, so confirm them on its pricing page.

| CRM | Free plan | Entry plan | Price (annual) |

## What 10 users pay per year

| CRM | Entry tier | Mid tier |
|---|---|---|
| Freshsales | $1,080 (Growth) | $4,680 (Pro, $39) |
| Zoho CRM | $1,680 (Standard) | $2,760 (Professional, $23) |
| Pipedrive | $1,680 (entry) | $4,680 (Growth, $39) |
| HubSpot | $1,800 (Starter) | $12,000 (Professional, $100) + one-time onboarding (about $1,500) |
| Salesforce | $3,000 (Starter Suite) | $12,000 (Pro Suite, $100) |

**The pattern:** entry prices sit within $16 of each other. Mid tiers differ by more than 4×, so price the tier you will actually need in a year.

## Costs beyond the seat price

1. **Onboarding fees:** HubSpot Professional adds a one-time onboarding fee.
2. **AI add-ons:** Zoho bundles its Zia assistant into plans and Freshsales includes Freddy AI features, but some AI usage is billed separately. Salesforce sells many AI and sales-engagement features as add-ons.
3. **Monthly billing:** HubSpot Starter is $20 monthly against $15 annually, 25% more.
4. **Implementation:** large Salesforce deployments often need a partner, which can cost more than the licenses in year one.

## Which CRM fits which budget

- **$0:** HubSpot Free, Zoho CRM Free (3 users) or Freshsales Free (3 users).
- **Lowest paid price:** Freshsales Growth.
- **Most features for the money:** Zoho CRM Professional.
- **Simplest pipeline for a small sales team:** Pipedrive.
- **Complex, large sales organization:** Salesforce Enterprise ($175).
