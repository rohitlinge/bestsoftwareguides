**Short answer:** GEO (generative engine optimization) is the work of getting your brand mentioned and cited in answers from AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overviews. It is measured by **asking those assistants the questions your buyers ask, many times, and counting** how often your brand appears, where it appears in the answer, which competitors appear instead and which web pages the AI cited. Tools that do this automatically are called AI visibility, GEO or AEO trackers; we compare 18 of them in our [AI search insights tools ranking](/best/ai-search-insights-tools/).

## How is GEO different from SEO?

| | SEO | GEO |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | Rank a page in search results | Get a brand named and cited in an AI answer |
| Unit you track | Keyword | Prompt (a full question) |
| Result | A position from 1 to 100 | Mentioned or not, position in the answer, cited sources |
| Stability | Positions change slowly | The same prompt can give a different answer each run |
| Main lever | Your own pages | Your pages plus the third-party pages the AI reads |

The two overlap. AI assistants that search the web usually read pages that already rank well, so good SEO helps GEO. The difference is that an AI answer often cites review sites, comparison articles, forums and news instead of your own site, so the pages you need to influence are frequently not yours.

## What do AI visibility tools measure?

Most trackers report the same core numbers:

- **Visibility or mention rate:** the share of answers that mention your brand.
- **Share of voice:** your mentions compared with each competitor's across the same prompts.
- **Average position:** how early in the answer your brand is named.
- **Citations:** which URLs and domains the AI cited, and how often each one appears.
- **Sentiment:** whether the answer describes you positively, neutrally or negatively (not every tool offers this).
- **Query fan-out:** the web searches the AI ran behind the scenes to build its answer. Some tools show these hidden queries.

Citations are the most actionable of these. A visibility score tells you there is a problem; the list of cited pages tells you which articles to improve, which review sites to get listed on and which authors to contact.

## Why do answers change every time?

AI answers are generated, not looked up, so the same prompt can name different brands on different runs, days and countries. Good trackers deal with this in two ways: they **repeat prompts** and report averages, and they let you set a **country or language** so results match your market. Treat a single run as an anecdote and a week of runs as data.

There is a second source of difference: **how the answer is collected**. Some tools call each AI's official API with web search turned on; others capture what the consumer app shows. API answers can differ from what a signed-in user sees in the ChatGPT or Gemini app, because the app adds memory, personalization and its own search settings. Ask each vendor which method it uses.

## Which AI engines should you track?

Start with where your buyers actually ask. For most brands that means ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews first, then Gemini, Perplexity and Claude. Engine coverage varies a lot by plan: several trackers include Claude only on Enterprise plans, and Google AI Overviews or AI Mode is missing from some tools entirely. ## How do you improve your AI visibility?

1. **Pick 25 to 50 real buyer questions**, such as "best CRM for a 10-person agency", not just your brand name.
2. **Run them for two weeks** across the engines that matter to you and record a baseline.
3. **Read the cited sources.** Note the review sites, listicles, forums and documentation pages that keep appearing.
4. **Fix your own pages** so they answer those questions directly, with names, numbers and dates that are easy to quote.
5. **Get onto the cited third-party pages** by correcting outdated facts, joining comparison lists and answering community threads honestly.
6. **Re-measure monthly.** Changes take weeks to show, because AI assistants pick up new pages only after they are crawled and indexed.

## How much does it cost to track AI visibility?

Paid trackers start at €29 to $100 a month for 16 to 50 prompts, and enterprise tools are quote-only. Free options exist: Semrush has a free account with a demo project, and [AI Search Inspector](/tools/ai-search-inspector/) is free open-source software where you pay the AI providers a few cents per answer.
