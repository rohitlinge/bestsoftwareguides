**Short answer:** Most VPN deals more than double at renewal. NordVPN Basic's 2-year plan works out at **$2.99 a month**, then renews at **$139.08 a year** ($11.59 a month), a 288% rise. IPVanish Essential rises 280%, ExpressVPN Basic 179% and Surfshark Starter 164%. **Private Internet Access** is the exception: its plans renew at the price you first paid. Over three years, PIA costs **$81.20** in total and NordVPN Basic **$219.81**. Prices were checked on each VPN's own pricing or checkout page on November 15, 2026, while Black Friday deals were live.

## How do VPN deals work?

A VPN's headline price is the monthly equivalent of a long plan paid up front, usually with bonus months added. When that first term ends, the plan renews automatically, in most cases **for one year at the regular price**, not for another two years at the deal price.

Our rankings list each VPN's **1-year plan** price as its annual price. This report looks at the longest common deal, the 2-year plan, and what happens after it.

## Is the 1-year plan ever the better deal?

Sometimes. During this promotion, Proton VPN Plus costs **$35.88 for the first year** ($2.99 a month), less per month than its 2-year deal ($3.49). Both renew at $83.88 a year. Over three years, though, the 2-year plan is still cheaper: $167.64, against $203.64 for the 1-year plan plus two renewals.

Private Internet Access's 1-year plan is $47.88 and renews at $47.88, so there is no renewal jump on either PIA plan.

## How do you avoid paying the renewal price?

1. **Note the renewal date** when you buy. The plan renews automatically at the price in the small print.
2. **Turn off auto-renew** in your account straight after buying. You keep the service until the term ends.
3. **Before the term ends, compare** the renewal price with a new-customer deal. Some VPNs offer existing customers a lower price if they cancel; none of the pricing pages we checked promises this.
4. **Use the refund window.** Most of these VPNs give 30 days, CyberGhost 45 and Mullvad 14.

## Which VPN is the best value?

- **Lowest cost over several years:** Private Internet Access, with no renewal increase.

- **Largest renewal jump:** NordVPN Basic, from $2.99 to $11.59 a month.
