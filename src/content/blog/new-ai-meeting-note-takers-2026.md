**Short answer:** Four AI note takers launched or relaunched between September 2025 and November 2026: [Wispr Flow Notetaker](/tools/wispr-flow-notetaker/) (August 2026), [Voibe Work](/tools/voibe-work/) (September 2026), [talat](/tools/talat/) (March 2026) and [TwinMind](/tools/twinmind/) (Android launch and seed round, September 2025). **None of them sends a bot into the meeting.** Each records audio on your own computer or phone. The cheapest paid option is talat at **$89 a year ($7.42 a month)**, or $189 for a lifetime licence. Wispr Flow and TwinMind have free plans that include meeting notes. Prices were read on each vendor's pricing page on November 22, 2026.

## What is new about these note takers?

Older tools such as [Otter.ai](/tools/otter-ai/) and [Fireflies.ai](/tools/fireflies-ai/) send a bot that joins the call as a participant. All four new tools record locally instead. They capture your microphone and the call's system audio, so other people see no extra attendee. This is the approach [Granola](/tools/granola/) made popular, and Granola is still #1 in our [AI meeting note taker ranking](/best/ai-meeting-note-takers/).

Two of the four come from dictation apps. Wispr Flow and Voibe both started as voice-typing tools and added meeting notes in 2026. talat and Voibe go furthest on privacy: talat transcribes on-device, and Voibe saves transcripts and summaries on your computer and deletes the meeting audio after processing.

## What does Wispr Flow Notetaker cost?

Notetaker is included in every Wispr Flow plan, including the free one, which adds speaker identification, questions about any meeting and MCP access, with a weekly meeting limit. Recordings under 5 minutes don't count against that limit. **Pro costs $15 per user a month, or $12 billed annually**, and removes the history limit. Growth ($18 a month annually) adds SAML SSO and a HIPAA BAA. Wispr lists SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 on every plan. There is no Android version yet. See [Wispr Flow Notetaker pricing](/tools/wispr-flow-notetaker/pricing/) or [Granola vs Wispr Flow Notetaker](/compare/granola-vs-wispr-flow-notetaker/).

## What does Voibe Work cost?

Voibe Work is the meeting-notes tier of the Voibe dictation app. It costs **$19 a month, $149 a year at list price, or $399 for a lifetime licence**. A 25% offer brought the yearly price to $111.75 when we checked. The 30-day trial needs no credit card. Voibe offers a BAA on request, but team admin features need Enterprise, which starts above 10 seats. It has no mobile app. See [Voibe Work pricing](/tools/voibe-work/pricing/).

## What does talat cost?

talat is a Mac-first app from Yorkshire, UK, that transcribes on your machine and summarizes with a local model or your own API key. It needs no account. It costs **$89 a year or $189 once**. The Teams plan is $89 per seat a year with a 3-seat minimum. The free trial covers 10 hours of recording. On Mac it needs Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later. It exports to Markdown, Obsidian and PDF and has a built-in MCP server. See [talat pricing](/tools/talat/pricing/).

## What does TwinMind cost?

TwinMind runs in the background on your phone, computer or browser and captures meetings and in-person conversations, then writes summaries and daily digests. Its free plan includes transcription, AI summaries and AI chat. **Pro is $11.99 a month billed annually**, and Max is $29.99. Monthly-billing prices aren't shown. It's the only one of the four with an Android app. SOC 2 is listed for Enterprise only. See [TwinMind pricing](/tools/twinmind/pricing/).

## Which new note taker should you pick?

- **You want a free plan that includes meeting notes:** Wispr Flow Notetaker or TwinMind.
- **You don't want a subscription:** talat ($189 lifetime) or Voibe Work ($399 lifetime).
- **Your notes must stay on your computer:** talat (on-device transcription) or Voibe Work (local storage).
- **You need Android:** TwinMind.
- **You need SSO or a HIPAA BAA:** Wispr Flow Growth. Voibe offers a BAA on request.
- **You need CRM sync for sales calls:** none of the four. See [AI note takers for sales teams](/best/ai-note-takers-for-sales-teams/).
