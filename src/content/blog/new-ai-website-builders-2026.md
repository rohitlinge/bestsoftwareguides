**Short answer:** Two big hosting companies launched AI website builders in November 2026: [Bluehost AI Builder](/tools/bluehost-ai-builder/) (November 10) and [Cloudways AI Website Builder](/tools/cloudways-ai-website-builder/) (November 16). They join three builders from the past 18 months: [Ploy](/tools/ploy/) (June 2026), [Figma Sites](/tools/figma-sites/) (open beta since May 2025) and [Readdy](/tools/readdy/) (April 2025). **Bluehost has the only free plan that publishes a site**, but on a subdomain that search engines don't index. For one site on your own domain, the cheapest is **Readdy Starter at $180 a year**, which includes a free domain for the first year. Prices were read on each vendor's pricing page on November 22, 2026.

## What do these builders have in common?

Each one, except Figma Sites, starts from a text prompt: you describe the business and the builder writes the pages. You then edit by chat, in a visual editor or, on some, in code. Most charge by AI credits, so heavy editing can cost more than the base plan. Figma Sites works the other way: it publishes designs you have already made in Figma.

## What is Bluehost AI Builder?

Bluehost AI Builder creates sites and simple web apps, with bookings, forms, a database, invoices and user accounts. The **free plan gives 5 credits a day (30 a month)** and publishes on a .app.bluehost.ai address that Bluehost says search engines don't index, so it suits testing, not a business site. Lite ($240 a year) adds a custom domain and 100 credits a month. Credits expire after 30 days, but a published site stays online if you run out. See [Bluehost AI Builder pricing](/tools/bluehost-ai-builder/pricing/) or [Wix vs Bluehost AI Builder](/compare/wix-vs-bluehost-ai-builder/).

## What is Cloudways AI Website Builder?

Cloudways, owned by DigitalOcean, builds a **standard WordPress or WooCommerce site** from native Gutenberg blocks. You keep full WordPress admin access, so you aren't locked into the builder. It's billed per site ($7.99, falling to $5.99 at 21+ sites), and hosting is a separate bill from $11 a month. Each site gets 20 AI credits a month, which don't roll over; edits in the visual and code editors use no credits. See [Cloudways AI Website Builder pricing](/tools/cloudways-ai-website-builder/pricing/) or [WordPress.com vs Cloudways](/compare/wordpress-com-vs-cloudways-ai-website-builder/).

## What are Readdy, Ploy and Figma Sites?

- **Readdy** builds and hosts sites with forms, sign-in pages, a database and a built-in Shop with checkout (Stripe, PayPal or Square). Yearly plans include one free custom domain for the first year. Monthly billing costs about two-thirds more ($25 vs $15 on Starter). See [Readdy pricing](/tools/readdy/pricing/).
- **Ploy**, founded by Webflow co-founder Bryant Chou, is a website builder and marketing platform: it hosts your site, identifies visitors, builds landing pages and manages Meta ads. It imports sites from WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace, Framer and Wix. It's priced for marketing teams, from $40 a month. See [Ploy pricing](/tools/ploy/pricing/).
- **Figma Sites** publishes Figma designs as responsive sites, with a CMS and no limit on site count. It's included with a paid Figma Full seat, so it's cheap if your team already pays for Figma. It's still in beta and has no code export or store. See [Framer vs Figma Sites](/compare/framer-vs-figma-sites/).

## Which new builder should you pick?

- **You want a WordPress site you can take elsewhere:** Cloudways.
- **The lowest first-year cost on your own domain:** Readdy Starter.
- **A small online store:** Readdy (Shop) or Cloudways (WooCommerce).
- **Your designers already work in Figma:** Figma Sites.
- **A B2B marketing team that also wants visitor data and ads:** Ploy.
- **A free test site before you pay:** Bluehost AI Builder.

## Which AI builders did we leave out?

- **Lovable and Bolt.new:** they launched in 2024 and are mainly app builders.
- **Wix Harmony:** launched in January 2026, but it's part of Wix plans, not sold separately.
- **Relume:** it makes sitemaps and wireframes but doesn't host sites.

## How did we check these builders?

We read each vendor's own pricing page and help pages on November 22, 2026, and listed every source on each tool's page. Where a page showed only a discounted or yearly price, we noted it in the plan details instead of guessing the monthly price.
