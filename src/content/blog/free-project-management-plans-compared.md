**Short answer:** **ClickUp** has the most generous free plan for teams (**unlimited members**). **Trello** and **Asana** cover up to **10 people** for free. **monday.com**'s free plan stops at **2 seats**, and **Notion**'s free plan is built for individuals.

## Free plans at a glance

| Tool | Free plan limit | First paid plan (annual) | Paid minimum |
|---|---|---|---|
| [ClickUp](/tools/clickup/) | Unlimited members, limited storage | $7/user/mo (Unlimited) | 1 seat |
| [Trello](/tools/trello/) | Up to 10 collaborators per workspace, 10 boards | $5/user/mo (Standard) | 1 seat |
| [Asana](/tools/asana/) | Personal plan, up to 10 users | $10.99/user/mo (Starter) | 1 seat |
| [monday.com](/tools/monday-com/) | Up to 2 seats | $9/seat/mo (Basic) | 3 seats |
| [Notion](/tools/notion/) | For individuals | $10/member/mo (Plus) | 1 seat |

Prices last checked November 14, 2026. Each tool page links to the vendor's pricing page.

## When the free plan is enough

- **A team of 2-10 on simple boards:** Trello Free or Asana Personal.
- **A growing team that wants docs and tasks in one place:** ClickUp Free Forever, because adding people does not force an upgrade.
- **A solo user organizing work and notes:** Notion Free.
- **Two people trying monday.com:** the free plan works. The third person means a paid plan with a 3-seat minimum.

## What you pay when you outgrow it

For a 5-person team on the first paid plan, billed annually:

| Tool | Cost per year |
|---|---|
| Trello Standard | $300 |
| ClickUp Unlimited | $420 |
| monday.com Basic | $540 |
| Notion Plus | $600 |
| Asana Starter | $659.40 |

Monthly billing adds 17-30% on these plans. Trello Standard is $6 monthly instead of $5, and ClickUp Unlimited is $10 instead of $7.

## The catch on each free plan

- **Trello:** timeline, calendar and dashboard views need Premium ($10/user/mo annually).
- **ClickUp:** storage is limited on the free plan, and the number of features takes time to learn.
- **Asana:** timeline and goals need paid plans. The jump from Starter to Advanced ($24.99) is steep.
- **monday.com:** 2 seats, and paid plans start at 3.
- **Notion:** collaboration and upload limits. The full AI features need Business ($20/member/mo annually).
