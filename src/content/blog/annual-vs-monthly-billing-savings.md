## When monthly billing still makes sense

- **You are still testing.** One or two months on monthly billing costs less than a year you do not use.
- **Your headcount changes a lot.** Annual plans usually lock in a seat count for the term.
- **The saving is small.** At 17% (Trello Standard), the flexibility may be worth more than $12 a year per user.

## When annual is clearly better

- **The gap is 30% or more** (Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, tl;dv, ClickUp). On a team of 10, Otter.ai Pro annual saves $1,039 a year.
- **You have already used the tool for a few months** and know it fits.
