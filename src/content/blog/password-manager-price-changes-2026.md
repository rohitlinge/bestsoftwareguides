**Short answer:** In early 2026, **1Password** raised its individual plan to about **$47.88 a year**, and **Bitwarden** raised Premium to about **$19.80 a year**, its first increase in years. **Proton Pass** cut Pass Plus from $3.99 to **$1.99 a month** on annual billing. **Dashlane** removed its free plan. Bitwarden is still the cheapest paid option, and Bitwarden and Proton Pass both keep strong free plans.

These prices are reported from published 2026 breakdowns (see each tool page for sources) and were last checked November 14, 2026. Always confirm the current and renewal price on the vendor's site.

## The changes

| Password manager | What changed in 2026 | Individual price now (annual) |
|---|---|---|
| [1Password](/tools/1password/pricing/) | Individual price increase (March 2026) | About $3.99/mo ($47.88/yr) |
| [Bitwarden](/tools/bitwarden/pricing/) | Premium price increase | About $1.65/mo ($19.80/yr) |
| [Proton Pass](/tools/proton-pass/pricing/) | Pass Plus price cut (March 2026) | $1.99/mo ($23.88/yr) |
| [Dashlane](/tools/dashlane/pricing/) | Free plan removed, personal plans restructured | About $4.99/mo ($59.88/yr) |
| [NordPass](/tools/nordpass/pricing/) | Promotional pricing continues | About $1.89/mo (promotional; renewal can be higher) |

## Who should switch, and who should not

- **On Dashlane Free:** that plan no longer exists. Bitwarden Free and Proton Pass Free are the closest no-cost replacements.
- **Paying for 1Password and happy with it:** the increase is about $1 a month. Its family sharing and polished apps are still why people stay.
- **Paying for Bitwarden Premium:** it is still the cheapest paid plan here, even after the increase.
- **Price-sensitive and privacy-minded:** Proton Pass Plus at $1.99 now costs about the same as Bitwarden Premium and adds built-in email aliases.

## Check the renewal price

Promotional first-term prices are common in this category, especially for NordPass. Before you buy, look for the renewal price on the checkout page, and compare plans on what they cost from year two.

## Features that did not change

All five support passkeys. Bitwarden and Proton Pass are open source, and only Bitwarden can be self-hosted. See the [password managers ranking](/best/password-managers/) for the full comparison.
